Privacy Policy for Ferry Friends!

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Ferry Friends! respects your privacy. This privacy policy explains how information is handled when you use the Ferry Friends! application.

Ferry Friends! does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information or user data.

1. Data Collection

Ferry Friends! does not collect personal information or other user data. The app does not require users to create an account, register, sign in, or provide names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other identifying information.

2. Analytics and Tracking

Ferry Friends! does not use advertising trackers, third-party tracking technologies, or analytics services that collect personal information.

The app does not track users across other apps, websites, or services.

3. Device and Technical Information

Ferry Friends! does not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, hardware identifiers, advertising identifiers, or other information that could be used to identify or track a user.

4. Location Information

Ferry Friends! does not access, collect, store, or transmit precise or approximate location information.

5. Contacts, Photos, Files, and Other Device Data

Ferry Friends! does not access or collect your contacts, photos, camera, microphone recordings, documents, messages, or other personal content stored on your device.

6. Data Sharing

Because Ferry Friends! does not collect personal information or user data, it does not sell, rent, disclose, or share personal information with third parties.

7. Advertising

Ferry Friends! does not display targeted advertising and does not provide personal information to advertising networks.

8. Children's Privacy

Ferry Friends! does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users.

Because the app does not collect personal information, no personal user data is stored or processed based on the user's age.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Ferry Friends! does not collect or store personal information on external servers. Therefore, there is no personal user data retained by the developer that requires deletion.

10. Third-Party Services

Ferry Friends! does not use third-party services that collect personal user information on behalf of the app.

If third-party services are introduced in a future version of the app, this privacy policy will be updated to describe the relevant data practices.

11. Security

Since Ferry Friends! does not collect or store personal information, the app does not maintain a database containing personal user data.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the features or privacy practices of Ferry Friends! change.

Any changes will be reflected on this page by updating the "Last updated" date shown above.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact the developer using the contact information provided on the Ferry Friends! App Store product page.
